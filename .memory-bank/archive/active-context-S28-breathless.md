The middle arc was the synthesis prompt. 0.75.0 forbade subject-widening after a graded run where nine of nine over-claims were the same operation, the predicate kept exactly and the subject enlarged. 0.76.0 paired it with an anti-transcription counterweight, measured over-claims 4 to 6, and was reverted whole as 0.76.1 on the reasoning that a correctness failure beats a quality failure. Two pre-existing bugs in that shipped path were fixed on the way, both able to write wrong text into a stack's `index.md`: an unbounded field scan that read body lines as frontmatter, and `dirname $0` breaking under symlinked invocation with a `|| true` masking it into silently-empty titles.
